About Onium

Connecting overseas recruitment
with university admissions.

ONIUM is an education-support company that manages overseas recruitment partners, promotes Korean universities and programs, pre-screens applicants, reviews admission documents, and supports admission and visa procedures. We help reduce repeated document requests and communication errors between overseas partners and universities.

01 · RECRUITMENTBroader recruitment reach

We connect universities with more prospective students through selected overseas partner networks.

02 · SCREENINGApplicant pre-screening

We review academic history, grades, language ability, study gaps, and basic communication skills before submission.

03 · DOCUMENTSFewer document errors

We check personal, academic, and financial documents against each university’s submission requirements.

04 · COORDINATIONKorea-based coordination

Universities communicate with ONIUM in Korea while we coordinate admissions work with overseas partners.

LANGUAGE TRAINING02

Korean & English Language Programs

D-4-1 · D-4-7

Based on the preferred language and start date, we review available schools and schedules and guide each stage from application to arrival.

VISA REVIEW ROUTEVisa review conducted in KoreaWe support the documents and procedures required for the Korea-based visa review connected to the admission process.
  1. 01
    School & intake reviewWe review Korean or English programs and available start dates.
  2. 02
    Application document preparationWe check each school’s requirements and submission documents.
  3. 03

  4. 04
